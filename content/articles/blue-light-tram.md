# Blue light at the last tram stop

At 11:42 PM, the depot switch clicks. The overhead wires hum in a low B-flat, vibrating in sync with the cold rain collecting on the shelter glass.

A person waiting alone on a wooden bench illuminated by blue fluorescent light does not look lonely; they look held in suspense, suspended between the labor of the day and whatever awaits behind closed apartment doors.

The camera shutter opens for one-fifth of a second. In that fraction, the train arrives not as steel and glass, but as a streak of warm gold erasing the blue.
