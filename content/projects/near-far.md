## The Problem

Modern messaging applications prioritize immediacy: typing bubbles, read receipts, and push alerts demand immediate cognitive attention. This urgency flattens thoughtful correspondence into fleeting snippets.

## Design Philosophy

*Near / Far* is an interface experiment structured around pacing:

- **Transmission Latency by Choice**: Letters simulate distance by arriving with a gentle delay calibrated to physical geography.
- **Reading Ritual**: Letters unfold without notifications or interruptions. The interface darkens everything else to focus attention on long-form paragraphs.
- **Archive by Return**: Correspondence is organized chronologically as a physical binding, turning digital conversations into living books.
