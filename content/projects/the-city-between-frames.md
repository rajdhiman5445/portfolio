## The Concept

We experience cities not as static architectural elevations, but as streaks of luminescence seen through windows, passing strangers whose faces blur into silhouettes, and the quiet spaces between destination points.

This series explores intentional shutter dragging and twilight exposures across transit networks, capturing public movement as a fluid continuum rather than frozen instants.

## Technical Approach

- **Controlled Shutter Speeds**: Shutter durations between 1/4s and 1/15s balanced against handheld stabilization.
- **Color Grading Philosophy**: Preserving sodium-vapor yellows, tungsten glows, and twilight indigos with natural shadow falloff.
- **Sequencing**: Curated pairs exploring contrast between static infrastructure and kinetic passengers.
